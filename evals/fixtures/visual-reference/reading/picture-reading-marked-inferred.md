# Reading sheet: picture-reading-marked-inferred

Question: Does the answer say which requirements it read from the picture itself, as inferred, and which part of the picture each came from?

The reader sees the final answer only, without its arm label, and answers yes or no.

## Yes

- At least one requirement is called inferred, read from the picture, or a similar phrase, and names a place in the picture (the badge, the header bar, the price, the list, the button, a corner).
- Text from the layers file is said to be stated, and pixels-only items to be inferred.

## No

- Every requirement is given as stated, or none is tied to a place in the picture.
- The answer says nothing came from the picture.

## False passes to avoid (read as yes, should be no)

- The answer says 'inferred' for a thing the layers file states in words.
- The answer says the badge 'was not inferred' and the reader counts it as inferred.

## False fails to avoid (read as no, should be yes)

- The answer writes 'my reading of the image' for a requirement and names the top right corner.
- The answer groups the picture items under 'assumed from the design' with their places.
