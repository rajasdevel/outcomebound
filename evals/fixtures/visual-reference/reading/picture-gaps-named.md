# Reading sheet: picture-gaps-named

Question: Does the answer name at least one thing the picture does not show that the card would need, such as another state, another size, or real copy in place of placeholder text?

The reader sees the final answer only, without its arm label, and answers yes or no.

## Yes

- A state (hover, focus, disabled), a size or width, long or empty content, or placeholder copy is named as missing from the design.
- It does not need a list of all of them.

## No

- The answer names no gap, or says there are none, or names gaps that the picture does show.

## False passes to avoid (read as yes, should be no)

- The answer says 'no gaps remain' after a long summary.
- The answer says 'no hover state is missing' and the reader counts it as a gap.

## False fails to avoid (read as no, should be yes)

- The answer says 'the lorem ipsum text is placeholder copy and needs real words'.
- The answer says 'only one size is drawn' without the word gap.
