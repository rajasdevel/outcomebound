How long each option lasts

The disk holds 500 GB and 410 GB of it is used. Growing it to 2 TB keeps every upload on the disk. Moving uploads older than 90 days to the archive keeps only the last three months on the disk. Uploads grow each month, and you may judge how fast differently from the log, so change the two guesses.

Months until the disk is full, for each option
OptionMonths until the disk is full
A grow the disk
B archive uploads older than 90 days
