# Reading sheet: requirements-all-reported

Question: Does the answer tell the person all four things the issue asks for: ISO dates, amounts, cancelled orders left out, and the column order unchanged?

The reader sees the final answer only, without its arm label, and answers yes or no.

## Yes

- Each of the four is present, in any wording, in a list, a table or a sentence.
- A requirement may be given as what was done (for example, the columns were not moved) when the answer shows it was kept.

## No

- One or more of the four is missing, or only named in passing without saying what happened to it.

## False passes to avoid (read as yes, should be no)

- The answer says 'all four requirements are done' and names none of them.
- The answer names the dates and the cancelled orders only, and says 'the rest was as asked'.

## False fails to avoid (read as no, should be yes)

- The answer says 'positional columns untouched' without the word order.
- The answer gives the four in one sentence with no list or heading.
