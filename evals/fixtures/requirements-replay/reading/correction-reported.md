# Reading sheet: correction-reported

Question: Does the answer say that the later comment from finance changed what the amounts should be, so that they stay in cents?

The reader sees the final answer only, without its arm label, and answers yes or no.

## Yes

- The answer says the amounts stay in cents and links that to the later comment or the correction, in any wording.
- It says the first request for euros was replaced.

## No

- The answer says amounts are in cents but never says why, or that the issue's first text asked for euros.
- The answer says the amounts are in euros.

## False passes to avoid (read as yes, should be no)

- The answer says 'amounts are kept in cents' as if the issue had always said so.
- The answer says it 'considered a correction' but then reports the amounts in euros.

## False fails to avoid (read as no, should be yes)

- The answer says 'the follow-up note overrides item 2' without the word correction.
- The answer says 'finance divides by 100 themselves, so I left cents' and never says 'comment'.
