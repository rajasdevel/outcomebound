What this page explains

For: anyone who builds or runs a service that reads from a database. After this page you can say what a cache does and estimate how much load it removes.

Cache
A small, fast store that keeps copies of recent answers.
Hit
A read that the cache answers from its copy.
Miss
A read the cache cannot answer, so it asks the database.
Hit rate
The share of reads that are hits.

One case first

A report page gets 120 reads each second. The cache answers 9 of every 10 of them. Only the misses reach the database: 120 reads times one tenth is 12 reads each second.

The rule: the database sees only the reads the cache misses.
